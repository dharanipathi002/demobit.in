About DEMOBIT

Hardware made for experimentation.

DEMOBIT is an electronics hardware company focused on designing and manufacturing affordable development boards, breakout boards and embedded hardware.

Our goal is simple: create dependable hardware that gives engineers, developers, makers and students the freedom to experiment, prototype and build without unnecessary cost or complexity.
